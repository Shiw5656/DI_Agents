### Documentation Gaps & Recommendations
1. **Business Key Enforcement**: Composite keys (e.g., CourseSectionBk) in Links like LnkEnrollment are not validated through controlled reference tables.
2. **Effective Dates**: Historization metadata lacks rules to validate overlapping or future date inconsistencies.
3. **Computed Logic**: Business Vault summaries require extended lineage definitions to support audit.
4. **Anomalies**:
   - Negative section capacities and grades exceeding known limits were flagged during profiling. Correct upstream data or ETL processes.
